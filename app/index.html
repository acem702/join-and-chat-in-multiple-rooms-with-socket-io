      Commands

      /join [room name] — create or join a room

      (Room names are 1 to 14 characters, using only letters, digits, underscores and Chinese characters)

      /ls — refresh the room list

      (The list refreshes automatically every 30 seconds, and immediately whenever anyone joins or leaves a room)

      Collapsing the room list

      (Use the « button at the top left of the room list to collapse or expand the right-hand panel)
