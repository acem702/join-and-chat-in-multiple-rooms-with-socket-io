      Tips

      Commands

      /join [room name]

      Create a room, or join it if it already exists. Names are 1 to 14 characters, using only letters, digits, underscores and Chinese characters.

      /ls

      Refresh the room list. It also refreshes on its own every 30 seconds, and immediately whenever anyone joins or leaves.

      Collapsing the room list

      Use the « button at the top of the room list to collapse or expand the right-hand panel.
